# EATM Placements

## Overview

EATM has a Training and Placement Cell that supports students in preparing for employment and connecting eligible students with recruiting organizations.

Placement information can change from one academic year to another. The assistant must therefore distinguish between general placement-cell information and current placement drives.

## Training and Placement Cell

The Training and Placement Cell is responsible for activities related to:

- Student training
- Placement preparation
- Industry interaction
- Recruitment drives
- Career guidance
- Internship-related opportunities
- Employability development

The placement process may include aptitude preparation, technical preparation, communication development, interview preparation, and interaction with recruiting organizations.

## Training Activities

EATM's institutional information describes training and skill-development activities intended to improve students' employability.

Depending on the academic year and programme, activities may include:

- Aptitude training
- Technical training
- Soft-skills development
- Communication-skills training
- Group discussions
- Personal interviews
- Career guidance
- Industry interaction
- Workshops and seminars

The exact training schedule should be verified from the latest Training and Placement Cell notice.

## Recruitment Drives

EATM conducts recruitment activities in which companies may visit the campus or recruitment opportunities may be communicated to eligible students.

The assistant must not assume that a particular company is currently recruiting unless a current verified placement notice has been added to the knowledge base.

## Placement Eligibility

Placement eligibility can depend on factors such as:

- Academic performance
- Backlogs
- Branch/programme
- Graduation requirements
- Company-specific criteria
- Other conditions specified by the recruiting organization

The assistant must not invent a universal placement eligibility rule.

For a specific recruitment drive, students should follow the eligibility criteria contained in the official company or Training and Placement Cell notification.

## Placement Process

A typical recruitment process may contain some or all of the following stages:

1. Registration
2. Eligibility screening
3. Pre-placement presentation or company briefing
4. Aptitude or online assessment
5. Technical assessment/interview
6. HR or personal interview
7. Selection/result announcement

The exact process varies between companies.

The assistant must not claim that every company follows all of these stages.

## Internships

Internship opportunities may be communicated through the Training and Placement Cell or through individual departments and organizations.

Internship eligibility, duration, stipend, selection process, and joining dates vary by organization.

The assistant must not invent internship opportunities or stipend amounts.

## Placement Statistics

Placement statistics are time-sensitive.

The assistant must not provide a placement percentage, average salary, highest salary, number of companies, or similar statistic unless the figure is available in a verified official EATM source and its academic year is clearly identified.

Older placement statistics must not be presented as current statistics.

## Current Placement Drives

No current company-specific recruitment drive is stored in this document.

If a student asks:

- "Which company is coming tomorrow?"
- "Which companies are hiring now?"
- "What is the current placement drive?"
- "What is the salary offered by a company?"
- "Am I eligible for today's drive?"

the assistant must not guess.

It should direct the student to the latest official placement notice or Training and Placement Cell communication unless a verified current notice has been added to the knowledge base.

## Student Preparation

Students preparing for placements may need to work on:

- Core technical subjects
- Programming
- Data structures and algorithms
- Aptitude
- Communication skills
- Resume preparation
- Technical interviews
- HR interviews
- Projects
- Internships and practical experience

These are general career-preparation areas and should not be presented as official EATM placement requirements unless supported by an official notice.

## Assistant Grounding Rule

Placement information is highly time-sensitive.

The assistant must distinguish between:

1. General Training and Placement Cell information.
2. Historical placement information.
3. Current recruitment notices.
4. Company-specific eligibility and selection criteria.

If current information is unavailable, the assistant must explicitly say that it is not available in the knowledge base rather than generating an answer.

The assistant must never fabricate:

- Company names
- Job roles
- Salary packages
- Placement percentages
- Selection results
- Recruitment dates
- Eligibility criteria
- Internship offers
