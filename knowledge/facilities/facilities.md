# EATM Facilities

## Overview

EATM provides academic, learning, recreational, and student-support facilities on its campus.

The official EATM website lists facilities including:

* Central Library
* Canteen and Cafeteria
* Hostel
* Transport
* Sports Area
* Internet and Intranet
* Health Centre

The institute also describes classrooms, laboratories, seminar halls, computer facilities, digital library resources, and other academic infrastructure.

## Central Library

EATM has a Central Library supporting students, faculty, and research activities.

The official library page states that the library provides lending, reference, current-awareness, and information services.

Library facilities include:

* Books and academic resources
* Journals and periodicals
* Digital resources
* OPAC/WEBOPAC for searching library resources
* Reading/reference facilities
* Computer-based digital library access

### Library Timings

The official library page currently lists:

* Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM
* Sunday and other holidays: Closed
* Issue/return/renewal: 9:45 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday to Saturday

The institution states that timings may change according to institutional requirements.

### Library Borrowing

The official library page currently lists:

* B.Tech students: up to 5 books for 14 days
* Diploma students: up to 5 books for 14 days
* M.Tech students: up to 4 books for 30 days
* Faculty: up to 4 books for 180 days
* Non-faculty: up to 2 books for 180 days

An overdue charge is specified on the official library page.

Students should verify the latest library rules because institutional policies and timings can change.

Official source:
https://www.eatm.in/central-library/
