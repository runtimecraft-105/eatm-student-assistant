# EATM Admissions

## Institution

Einstein Academy of Technology and Management (EATM) is located at Baniatangi, Khurda, Odisha.

EATM's official website provides information about its engineering programmes and admission-related processes.

## Admission Information

Admission requirements, eligibility criteria, available programmes, seat intake, fees, counselling procedures, and important dates can change from year to year.

The EATM Student Assistant must therefore avoid presenting old admission information as the current admission policy.

Students should verify the latest admission notification and eligibility requirements through the official EATM website, the applicable counselling authority, or the college admission office.

## B.Tech Programmes

EATM's official website lists undergraduate engineering programmes including:

* Computer Science and Engineering
* Electronics and Communication Engineering
* Electrical Engineering
* Electrical and Electronics Engineering
* Civil Engineering
* Mechanical Engineering

The current availability and intake of individual programmes should be verified from the latest official admission information.

## Admission and Counselling

EATM has an official Admission and Counselling Committee.

Admission-related activities may involve counselling, document verification, eligibility checking, registration, and other procedures prescribed by the applicable authorities.

Students should follow the latest official admission notification for the applicable admission year.

## Lateral Entry

EATM's official information describes lateral-entry admission opportunities for eligible students.

The applicable eligibility requirements, number of seats, and admission procedure should be verified from the latest notification because these details may change.

## Documents

Students seeking admission should follow the document requirements specified in the current admission notification.

The assistant must not provide a fixed list of required documents unless that list has been verified against the current official admission instructions.

## Fees

The assistant must not invent or provide outdated tuition fees, hostel fees, examination fees, or other charges.

For questions such as:

* "What is the current B.Tech fee?"
* "How much is hostel fee?"
* "What is the admission fee?"
* "How much do I have to pay?"

the assistant should direct the student to the latest official fee/admission information if the current amount is not present in the knowledge base.

## Important Dates

No current admission calendar is stored in this document.

The assistant must not guess:

* Application deadlines
* Counselling dates
* Admission reporting dates
* Document verification dates
* Fee-payment deadlines

Students should check the latest official EATM admission notice.

## Assistant Grounding Rule

Admission information is time-sensitive.

If the knowledge base does not contain a verified current admission detail, the assistant should clearly state that the information is not currently available and direct the student to the official EATM admission information or college administration.

The assistant must never fabricate admission eligibility, fees, deadlines, seat availability, or counselling results.
