# EATM Departments

## Institution

Einstein Academy of Technology and Management (EATM) is located at Baniatangi, Khurda, Odisha.

This document summarizes department and programme information available on the official EATM website. Students should verify current programme availability, intake, faculty, and other academic details from the official EATM website or college office because information can change.

## Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering was established in 2009. The department covers areas related to computing, information technology, software design, and applications.

Official EATM information describes CSE as a core department and mentions academic, research, placement, entrepreneurship, and industry-oriented activities.

Official source:
https://www.eatm.in/computer-science-and-engineering/

## Computer Science and Engineering – Data Science

EATM has a Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) programme/department page on its official website. The programme information discusses computing, information technology, software, and related academic areas.

Official source:
https://www.eatm.in/computer-science-and-engineering-data-science/

## Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering was established in 2009.

The official EATM website states that the B.Tech. ECE programme has an intake of 60. The department covers areas including electronic devices and circuits, communication systems, analog and digital transmission, microprocessors, satellite communication, antennas, microwave systems, networking, radar, and communication signal processing.

Official source:
https://eatm.in/electronics-and-communication-engineering/

## Basic Sciences and Humanities (BSH)

The Department of Basic Sciences and Humanities provides foundational education for engineering students.

The official EATM website states that the department teaches subjects including Engineering Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Business Economics, and Life Skills. It also provides laboratory and language-related learning support.

The department also describes activities related to communication skills, group discussions, presentations, interview skills, workshops, seminars, and industrial visits.

Official source:
https://www.eatm.in/basic-sciences-and-humanities/
