# EATM Examination Information

## Important Notice

Examination schedules, subjects, dates, rooms, and other operational details can change. The EATM Student Assistant should not invent or assume current examination schedules.

Students should verify the latest examination notice or contact the examination section/college administration for current schedules.

## Semester Examination

According to the official EATM Rules & Regulations:

* An end-semester examination is conducted at the end of each semester.
* The examination is conducted by the University according to the academic schedule announced for the academic year.
* Semester examination requirements and credit points are governed by the rules formulated by the Academic Council from time to time.

## Back Paper Examination

Students who have backlog subjects can appear for back paper examinations.

According to the official rules:

* Back paper examinations, when applicable, are held along with the normal end-semester examination.
* Students with backlogs can clear their backlog subjects with regular students of lower semesters in subsequent years.
* If a subject is offered in both semesters, the backlog may be cleared in the applicable semester according to the rules.

## Special Examination

EATM's official examination rules describe a Special Examination for eligible students after the final semester examination.

According to the published rules:

* Special examinations are conducted after the final semester examination for eligible subjects from the 3rd to final semester.
* Students who have completed the final semester examination may be eligible.
* Students who receive an F grade in eligible subjects may use the Special Examination opportunity to clear those subjects.
* Students may register for failed subjects according to the applicable examination rules.
* The Special Examination is stated to start after 30 June every year.
* Special examinations are conducted only at selected centres.

Students should verify the current university/college notification because examination regulations may be updated.

## Internal Examination

The official EATM Rules & Regulations page states that there are two internal examinations per semester.

According to the published rules:

* Number of internal examinations: 2
* Duration of each internal examination: 1 hour
* Each internal examination carries 15 marks.
* The internal examination question paper has:

  * 6 short-type questions × 2 marks = 12 marks
  * 3 long-type questions × 6 marks = 18 marks
  * Total question-paper marks = 30 marks

The official rules state that the awarded mark is calculated from 15 marks for each internal examination.

## Other Internal Assessment Components

The official rules also list the following assessment components:

* Surprise Test: 5 marks
* Quiz Test: 5 marks
* Assignment: 5 marks
* Attendance: 5 marks

Students should consult the latest academic/examination instructions for the current method of calculating internal marks.

## Examination Hall Guidance

EATM's official examination rules provide guidance for students during examinations, including:

* Remain attentive and avoid distractions.
* Carry the necessary examination materials.
* Write answers clearly and neatly.
* Focus on the quality and relevance of answers.
* Check grammar and spelling where applicable.
* Allocate time according to the marks assigned to questions.
* Review the answer paper before submitting it.

## Current Examination Schedule

No current examination timetable is stored in this knowledge document.

When a student asks:

"Which exam is tomorrow?"

"When is my semester exam?"

"What is my exam time?"

or another question requiring a current timetable, the assistant must not guess.

It should tell the student to check the latest official EATM examination notice or contact the examination section unless a current verified notice has been added to the knowledge base.

## Assistant Grounding Rule

The assistant must distinguish between:

1. General examination rules stored in this document.
2. Current examination schedules contained in official notices.
3. Information that is not available in the knowledge base.

If current information is unavailable, the assistant must clearly say so rather than generating an answer from assumptions.
