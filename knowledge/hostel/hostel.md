# EATM Hostel

## Overview

EATM provides hostel facilities for both boys and girls.

The official EATM hostel page describes separate hostels for boys and girls and states that catering facilities are provided.

Official source:
https://eatm.in/hostel/

## Hostel Facilities

According to the official EATM hostel information, hostel facilities include:

* Separate hostels for boys and girls
* Catering facilities
* Hostel superintendents/wardens to look after boarders
* Telephone facility
* Television facility
* Aquaguard/water purification facility
* Medical support through an appointed medical officer

An official EATM institutional document also describes hostel facilities for outstation students, hygiene and cleanliness measures, security personnel, CCTV surveillance, restricted access, medical facilities, and ambulance availability for hostel residents.

Official institutional source:
https://eatm.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/7.3.1_QlM_1.pdf

## Hostel Admission

Students seeking hostel accommodation are required to submit the Hostel Admission Form.

According to the official hostel page, applicants should personally report to the warden along with their parent/local guardian after paying the applicable hostel fees.

Students should verify the current hostel admission procedure and fee requirements with the college before making any payment.

## Hostel Rules

The official EATM hostel page specifies rules for residents.

### Leaving and Returning

Hostel residents are required to sign a register when leaving and returning to the hostel premises, except during college working hours.

The register is used to record relevant details of the student's movement.

The official page specifies disciplinary consequences for leaving without following the required procedure.

### Leaving During College Hours

Residents are not permitted to leave during college hours without permission from the Principal or Head of Department.

An application for station leave is required when leaving through the main gate.

### Return Time and Attendance

Residents must return to the hostel according to the prescribed hostel timing and attend the daily roll call.

Prior official permission or approved overnight leave may provide exceptions.

Students are not permitted to leave the hostel after attendance without authorization.

### Leaving the Station

A resident who leaves the station without prior permission from the warden may face disciplinary action.

The official rules state that a later letter from a parent or guardian does not substitute for prior permission.

### Visitors

Visitors certified by the parents may be permitted to meet hostel residents.

The official hostel rules require photographs of certified visitors to be submitted.

### Discipline

Misconduct or violation of hostel rules may be referred to the Disciplinary Committee.

Serious or repeated violations can result in disciplinary action, including expulsion from the hostel.

## Hostel Fees

The current hostel fee amount is not stored in this knowledge document.

The assistant must not guess hostel fees.

If a student asks:

* "How much is hostel fee?"
* "What is the hostel charge?"
* "How much do I have to pay for hostel?"

the assistant should direct the student to the latest official EATM hostel/admission information or hostel administration.

## Hostel Timings

The exact current hostel entry/return timing is not stored in this knowledge document.

The assistant must not invent a hostel curfew or return time.

Students should confirm the current timing with the hostel warden.

## Room Availability

Current hostel room availability and allocation are not stored in this knowledge base.

The assistant must not claim that a hostel room is available or unavailable unless a current verified notice has been added.

Students should contact the hostel administration for availability.

## Medical Support

The official EATM hostel page states that a medical officer is appointed to take care of students.

An official institutional document additionally describes medical facilities for hostel residents and ambulance availability.

Current emergency contact numbers, medical officer schedules, and ambulance procedures are not stored here and should be verified with the college.

## Important Assistant Rule

Hostel information can change.

The assistant may provide the documented hostel facilities and rules above, but it must not invent:

* Current hostel fees
* Room availability
* Room allocation
* Current curfew/return time
* Current warden contact numbers
* Current hostel admission deadlines
* Current meal charges
* Current hostel capacity

For such questions, the assistant should clearly state that current information is unavailable in the knowledge base and direct the student to the hostel administration.
