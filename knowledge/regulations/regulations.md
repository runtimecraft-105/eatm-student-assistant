# EATM Rules and Regulations

## Important Notice

College rules and regulations may be updated by the institution or the applicable university.

The EATM Student Assistant must use verified information from official EATM sources and must not invent disciplinary rules, academic requirements, penalties, or procedures.

## Academic Regulations

EATM's official Rules & Regulations page states that academic and examination matters are governed by rules formulated by the Academic Council and applicable university regulations.

Students should follow the latest official academic notifications for current requirements.

## Semester Examinations

According to the official EATM Rules & Regulations:

* An end-semester examination is conducted at the end of each semester.
* The examination is conducted according to the academic schedule announced for the academic year.
* Back-paper examinations, where applicable, are held with the normal end-semester examination.
* Students with backlogs are required to clear their backlog subjects according to the applicable examination rules.

## Special Examination

The official EATM rules describe special examinations for eligible students after the final semester examination.

Eligibility and registration requirements are governed by the published examination regulations.

Students should verify the latest examination notification before relying on the procedure or eligibility requirements.

## Internal Assessment

The official EATM Rules & Regulations describe internal examinations and other internal assessment components.

The published rules include:

* Two internal examinations per semester
* Surprise tests
* Quiz tests
* Assignments
* Attendance

The exact marks and assessment procedure should be checked against the latest official examination rules.

## Student Discipline

Students are expected to follow the academic, administrative, hostel, examination, and campus rules applicable to them.

Violations may result in disciplinary action according to the applicable institutional regulations.

The assistant must not determine or predict a disciplinary punishment for an individual student.

For a specific disciplinary matter, students should contact the appropriate college authority.

## Anti-Ragging

EATM publishes information relating to UGC regulations on curbing ragging in higher educational institutions.

The published material describes ragging as prohibited conduct and provides information about prevention and disciplinary consequences under the applicable regulations.

Students should follow the current anti-ragging rules and reporting procedures published by the institution.

Official source:
https://eatm.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/5.1.4_Final.pdf

## Examination Conduct

Students are required to follow examination-hall instructions and applicable examination regulations.

The official EATM examination rules provide guidance concerning examination conduct, preparation, and submission of answer scripts.

Students must follow the instructions given by the invigilator during an examination.

Official source:
https://eatm.in/rules-regulations/

## Hostel Regulations

Hostel residents must follow the hostel rules issued by EATM.

These include requirements concerning:

* Signing the hostel movement register
* Permission to leave during college hours
* Station leave
* Returning to the hostel
* Attendance/roll call
* Visitors
* Discipline

Current hostel procedures should be verified with the hostel administration.

Official source:
https://eatm.in/hostel/

## Attendance

Attendance requirements can be governed by academic and university regulations.

The assistant must not invent a minimum attendance percentage unless the relevant current regulation has been verified and added to the knowledge base.

If a student asks about their personal attendance status, the assistant should explain that it does not have access to the student's private academic record unless such data has been explicitly integrated into the system.

## Academic Misconduct

Students must comply with examination and academic integrity rules.

The assistant must not provide instructions for cheating, examination misconduct, plagiarism, impersonation, or other violations of academic rules.

For questions concerning a specific academic misconduct case, students should consult the appropriate college authority.

## Personal Student Records

The EATM Student Assistant does not have direct access to confidential student records in this prototype.

The assistant must not claim to know:

* A student's attendance
* Internal marks
* Examination results
* Backlog status
* Fee balance
* Hostel allocation
* Disciplinary record
* Personal academic information

unless a properly authorized student-information system has been integrated.

## Important Assistant Rule

When answering regulation-related questions:

1. Use only verified information from the knowledge base.
2. Do not invent penalties or procedures.
3. Do not treat historical rules as automatically current.
4. Clearly identify when current information is unavailable.
5. Direct students to the appropriate college authority for individual cases.
